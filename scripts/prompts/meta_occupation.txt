You classify Wikidata occupations (P106 values) into a meta occupation.
Each occupation comes with its qid, its label and the number of individuals
holding it. Classify the occupation itself, from its label.

The five meta occupations follow the level-1 domains of the Cross-Verified
database (Laouenan et al., 2022); the groups under each are its level-2 domains.

Culture
    Culture-core: literature, poetry, music (composers, singers,
    instrumentalists), painting, sculpture, theatre, film, dance, photography,
    comics, crafts practised as art.
    Culture-periphery: journalism, architecture, design, fashion, modelling,
    illustration, engraving, publishing, editing, radio and television
    presenting, beauty pageants.

Discovery/Science
    Academia: every scientist, scholar, researcher, university teacher,
    teacher at any level, educator, physician, nurse, historian, philosopher,
    theologian, economist.
    Explorer/Inventor/Developer: engineers, explorers, inventors, navigators,
    programmers, astronauts, conquistadors, settlers.

Leadership
    Politics: politicians, heads of state and government, ministers,
    ambassadors, members of parliament, activists, trade unionists.
    Administration/Law: lawyers, judges, diplomats, civil servants, police.
    Religious: clergy, monks, nuns, bishops, popes, rabbis, imams, missionaries,
    saints, religious leaders.
    Military: soldiers, officers, generals, admirals, pilots of war, samurai.
    Corporate/Executive/Business (large): businesspeople, entrepreneurs,
    bankers, merchants, executives, industrialists.
    Nobility: monarchs, emperors, princes, aristocrats, nobles, socialites.

Sports/Games
    Every athlete and player of a sport, coaches, referees, managers of sports
    teams, chess and other game players, esports, racing drivers, jockeys.

Other
    Worker/Business (small): farmers, shopkeepers, craftsmen, cooks,
    translators, librarians, courtiers, workers, servants.
    Family: relatives defined by kinship (wife of, son of, consort).
    Other: criminals, victims, prisoners, slaves, hobbyists, and labels that are
    not occupations at all (a place, an institution, an object, an event).

Rules
- One meta occupation per occupation, the domain of its main activity.
- A specific office goes with its domain: "ambassador of Italy to the United
  Kingdom" is Leadership, "court painter" is Culture, "team physician" is
  Discovery/Science.
- A field of study given instead of a person is classified by the field:
  "physics" is Discovery/Science. A label that names no activity is Other.
- Labels in other languages are read in their language.

Input: a JSON list of {"qid", "label", "individuals"}.
Output: STRICT JSON, a list with one object per input occupation, in the same
order, every qid kept:
[
  {"qid": "Q...", "label": "...", "meta_occupation": "Culture"},
  ...
]
meta_occupation is exactly one of: "Culture", "Discovery/Science",
"Leadership", "Sports/Games", "Other". No prose outside the JSON.
